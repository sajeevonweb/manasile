# Manasile

Manasile is a free, privacy-first assessment platform. It currently offers assessments based on the Five-Factor Model (FFM), with no sign-up or email registration required. All responses and results are stored locally on the user's device and are never uploaded, ensuring complete privacy.
## Current Assessments

### Big Five Personality Assessment

Measures five broad personality traits:

- Openness
- Conscientiousness
- Extraversion
- Agreeableness
- Neuroticism

Available in three versions:

- **Short** – 25 questions
- **Standard** – 50 questions
- **Comprehensive** – 120 questions

## Privacy & Security

- **No Sign-Up or Email Required:** Start taking the assessment instantly without registering.
- **Optional Local Nickname:** Users can enter a nickname to personalize results, but this information is stored purely in the local browser.
- **Zero Databases:** We do not run any backend servers or databases. Your data cannot be tracked, leaked, or sold because we never receive it.
- **Local Storage:** All responses and results are saved in the client's local browser storage.
