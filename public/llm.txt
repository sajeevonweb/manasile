# Manasile

Manasile is a free, privacy-first assessment platform. It currently offers Big Five personality assessments based on the Five-Factor Model (FFM), with additional assessments planned in the future. No account is required, and all assessment data remains on the user's device.

## Current Assessments

### Big Five Personality Assessment

Measures five broad personality traits:

- Openness
- Conscientiousness
- Extraversion
- Agreeableness
- Neuroticism

Available in three versions:

- **Short** – 25 questions
- **Standard** – 50 questions
- **Comprehensive** – 120 questions

## Privacy

- No sign-up required
- No user accounts
- No tracking or advertising
- All responses and results are stored locally on the user's device
